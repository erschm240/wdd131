What is The Choice Is Yours?

It's a site that contains Choose Your Own Adventure games for people to play. We enjoy playing these games and want to share them with others!

What is a "Choose Your Own Adventure" story?

It's an interactive adventure where you choose what happens. You follow a storyline of a character (or you are the character) and make choices based on the ones presented to you. You decide how the story progresses and what the final ending is! A story like this always has several endings, and it's up to you to find them all.
