In the Suburbs

The Adventure Begins

You are walking down a residential street. You live in a small suburban area and are going home about 2:00 pm after work. You rent a duplex with a couple of roommates, but they aren’t with you, so you’re walking alone. As you reach your home, you hear rustling and see a dark blur dive behind your neighbor’s side of the house. They don’t have any pets. Do you INVESTIGATE or continue HOME?
